# Credits

Generated by `bun run assets:credits` from `MESH_ASSETS` in
`src/render3d/assets.ts`. Do not edit by hand - add the asset to the manifest
and regenerate, which is what `cli assets` checks.

## 3D models

- **yellow_barrel** - "Anna Denisova (@Den1121)", [source](https://sketchfab.com/3d-models/low-poly-closed-barrels-8df46c47099a4b9d9bc4a69edcad1b88), CC BY 4.0

## Everything else

Surface textures are generated procedurally in `src/render3d/assets.ts` and are
not derived from any third-party asset. The physics, rendering and tooling are
original, ported from a C#/Godot prototype in this project's own history.
