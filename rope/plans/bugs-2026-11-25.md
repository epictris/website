# Bugs found on 2026-11-25

Found during the Decorations work of that day (brush cursor, right-click grow, Select tool, placed-decoration gizmo, texture layers; see [dirt-paint.md](dirt-paint.md) and docs/formations.md "Texture layers").
All of that work is uncommitted and unplayed at the time of writing.
Open bugs first, each with how to see it; then the ones fixed that day, for the record.

## Open

### 1. No decoration build reaches production

Nothing ships a decoration's build: neither `decoration.glb` (ferns, vines, mound moss, ivy) nor `layer.png` (texture moss).

- The vite build keeps only the files `levelFormationFiles` lists (`src/render3d/levelAssets.ts`), which are each body's formation file, and deletes every other file under `generated/` from the output (`vite.config.ts`, the `formations:` pass after the build).
- No publish script names a decoration file either (`grep -rn "decoration.glb\|decorationFile(" scripts src` finds only the builder, the dev store route and the renderer).
- Seen by reading the code, not by a production run: confirm with `bun run build` and look for `generated/<decoration hash>/` in the output.
- Fix direction: `levelFormationFiles` (or a sibling) also lists each grown decoration's file (`decorationFile` or `decorationLayerFile` by `isTextureLayer`), and the store bundles and `just publish` carry them as they carry the rock's.

### 2. 119 WebGL errors on every editor load

Every load of the editor on `ball.json` logs 119 of:

```
GL_INVALID_OPERATION: glDrawElements: Mismatch between texture format and sampler type (signed/unsigned/float/shadow).
```

- Seen in a headless Chromium 142 (`--use-gl=angle --use-angle=gl-egl`, display-less), counted in its stderr: 119 on a plain copy of `ball.json`, the same with or without that day's texture layers, and 0 on a bare scratch page drawing only formation rocks.
- Not yet seen on a real GPU: check Tris's Chrome console (claude-in-chrome `read_console_messages`, pattern `Mismatch`) before chasing it, in case it is ANGLE-on-EGL only.
- Likely suspects: a sampler declared `sampler2DShadow` or an integer sampler bound to an ordinary texture (a shadow map, the reflection probe, the cave light bake's textures, the depth prepass), or a float texture sampled where filtering is not supported.
- How to find which draw: patch `WebGL2RenderingContext.prototype.drawElements` over CDP to call `getError()` after each draw and log the current program's name (three's `renderer.info.programs`), as in memory reference_webgl_link_counter.

### 3. One frame drawn about 10% dark (not reproduced)

Once, after painting a new moss on a bare rock and right-clicking to grow it, the editor's whole view drew about 10% darker (mean luminance 35.4 down to 31.1) and stayed so until a reload.

- Not reproduced afterwards: painting on another bare rock, painting on existing moss, and a right-click alone all held the brightness (34.1 to 34.7).
- It happened in a headless Chromium whose GPU process had been restarted earlier in that session ("Context was lost", after a reload landed mid-load), so it may be that and nothing of the editor's.
- Worth one look in Tris's real browser: paint a new patch, right-click, and watch the exposure.

### 4. Every existing rock reads "Built by an older generator"

Not a defect but a consequence to know: `formation_export.py` joined the generators' fingerprint (`tools/blender/formations/worker.py`) on 2026-11-25, so every rock built before reads "Built by an older generator" in its panel.
Regenerate now rebuilds such a rock (it used to rebuild nothing, see Fixed 5), which gives it the paint UV texture layers need; it also drops the rock's bake, so bake it again after.
"Generate N out of date" counts stale recipes only, not older generators, so nothing regenerates every rock at once; that is deliberate (a regenerate of every rock is a long job and drops every bake), and a button for it is Tris's call.

### 5. Old texture mosses draw nothing until grown again

A texture-only moss grown before 2026-11-25 was a shell; its request now carries `layer: true`, so it is stale and its old build (a `decoration.glb`) is not looked for any more.
Until it is grown again (right-click with its brush, or Grow) it draws nothing, and on a rock without the paint UV its growth fails with "Generate it again to paint one on it".

## Fixed on 2026-11-25

1. **Texture moss had holes where the rock stood through it.**
   The 3 mm shell was built on another triangulation of the rock than the one drawn.
   Fixed by making a texture moss part of the rock's own look (docs/formations.md "Texture layers").
2. **The optimiser took the rock's paint UV out of every baked tier.**
   `gltf-transform optimize` prunes vertex attributes no material reads and renumbers the rest, so the bake's UV became TEXCOORD_0 in its place.
   Fixed with `optimize-asset.ts --keep-attributes`, passed for formation bakes (`scripts/blendExport.ts`).
3. **The crease rebuild could not carry a UV map** ("the crease rebuild cannot carry CORNER attribute 'PaintLayer' (FLOAT2)").
   Fixed by rebuilding once, at Generate, on the rock itself, and keeping the bows on it (`scene_export.keep_bows`, `REBUILT_BOWS`), so later copies are never rebuilt under the paint UV.
4. **A decoration's turn and size were dropped on save.**
   `scaleLevelData` lists a decoration's fields one by one and the new `turn` and `scale` were not among them, so a fern turned by its gizmo came back unturned on reload.
   Fixed in `src/level/levelFormat.ts`; case "a decoration's turn and size cross the disk unscaled" in `scripts/test-formations.ts`, proven red without the fix.
5. **Regenerate rebuilt nothing for an unchanged recipe**, though the panel promised "Regenerate builds it with the current code".
   The job runner answered from the store whatever generator built it.
   Fixed: a store entry built by another fingerprint is a miss and is built again with `--force` (`src/server/formations.ts`, `olderBuild`).
6. **The paint coverage dots ignored a changed threshold.**
   `PaintCoverage.update` returned early when no stamp was new, so a changed `threshold` setting kept the old region.
   Fixed in `src/editor/paintCoverage.ts`.
7. **The dev store did not serve `layer.png`**: the request fell through to the page's HTML.
   Fixed in `STORE_FILES` (`src/server/formations.ts`).
8. **An erase footprint was hidden under the coverage dots.**
   three sorts a group's children by the group's `renderOrder`; the cursor's group was 0 against the coverage's 12.
   Fixed in `src/editor/paintCursor.ts`.
9. **A selected decoration carried meaningless 2D handles**: the corner and turn handles of its 15 cm placeholder rect, standing on its point carried onto the plane, and a multi-selection of decorations got the shared handles too.
   Fixed: no 2D handles for a decoration (`computeHandles`), no shared gizmo for decorations alone (`gizmoSpec`).
10. **F on a decoration framed its 15 cm mark** at the wrong depth, coming in to a few centimetres of a fern's root.
    Fixed: it frames what the scene draws of it (`drawnBox`).
