# Methodology

1. Introduce mechanic
  - provide a scenario where the player would naturally encounter the mechanic required for the skill
2. Encourage experimentation
  - provide an environment where the player can test out the mechanic without the risk of losing progress
3. Low-consequence skill check
  - to progress, a specific skill must be performed
  - failing to perform the skill should not cause the player to lose progress (or only lose a small amount of progress)
4. Encourage mastery
  - provide an environment where the player can refine the skill in a variety of contexts
5. High-consequence skill check
  - to progress, the skill must be performed
  - failing to perform the skill should cause the player to lose a significant amount of progress (taking them to the beginning of the skill introduction area)

# Levels

## Sewers

1. Player lands in sewers
  - encourage player to continue moving right by entering already moving to the right
2. **MECHANIC: Hook Range**
  - nearest ceiling just outside hook range -> player learns hook range by trying/failing to attach
3. **MECHANIC: Swing**
  - sloped ground allows unobstructed swing
  - flat ceiling allows easy experimentation
  - recovery zone below so mistakes aren't punished
4. **MECHANIC: Chain Wrap**
  - sloped ceiling requires player to move closer to the ceiling during swing
5. **MECHANIC: Mid-Swing Hook**
  - gap spaced barely greater than hook range requires player to deploy hook while swinging
  - BONUS: height difference teaches player that hook bounces off impermeable walls
  - BONUS: height difference demonstrates momentum generated by swinging from 90 degrees
6. **FEATURE: Background anchor geometry**
  - vertical chamber encourages player to progress up
  - only one accessible attachment point - a background anchor
  - player must attach to first anchor to reach second anchor
  - player must swing through first anchor to reach third anchor
7. **MECHANIC: >90 degree swing**
  - anchor point placed above second anchor requires player to deploy hook while above their anchor point
  - recovery zone below allows experimentation without punishing mistakes
  - subsequent swings teach player to implement mechanic in constrained environments, with non anchor geometry, and with more precise aim requirements
  - skill check sends player back to beginning if they fail the final swing
